### My Favorite Series: Money Heist (La Casa de Papel)

**Money Heist** is a Spanish heist crime drama series that follows *The Professor* and his team of eight robbers.

> The Professor's plan: Print billions of euros in the Royal Mint of Spain.

#### Key Characters
- **The Professor** - The mastermind behind the heist.
- **Tokyo** - The narrator of the story.
- **Berlin** - The charismatic leader inside the mint.
- **Nairobi** - The expert forger and quality controller.

#### Why I Love It
1.  The intricate and intelligent plot.
2.  The deep and complex character development.
3.  The constant suspense and unexpected twists.
4.  The iconic theme song, *"Bella Ciao."*

You can learn more on the `[official Netflix page](https://www.netflix.com/title/80192098)`.
